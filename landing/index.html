About the role

We are a rapidly growing company at the forefront of innovation in the EdTech industry. Our team is dedicated to shaping the future of education through groundbreaking products and services. As we continue to expand, we are seeking a talented and experienced Creative Producer to join our dynamic team.

As a Creative Producer, you will play a crucial role in driving the creative vision and strategy for our EdTech product. You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to develop and implement innovative marketing campaigns that resonate with our target audience and drive user acquisition.

What you'll do

  • Set and execute social media and communication campaigns in alignment with product, research, and marketing strategies.
  • Lead brainstorming sessions and team activities to generate creative ideas and concepts.
  • Write comprehensive briefs for motion/graphic designers and content creators.
  • Review and approve advertising creatives, providing edits and feedback as needed.
  • Adapt advertising creatives for different platforms and target audiences.
  • Analyze the effectiveness of advertising creatives and propose enhancements based on findings.
  • Conduct market and competitor analysis to identify trends and opportunities.
  • Research and implement new tools and approaches to optimize the creative process and drive results.

What we're looking for

  • Minimum of 3 years of experience in positions such as Creative Specialist, Digital Marketing Manager, or Performance Marketing.
  • Proven experience working with B2C mobile applications.
  • Proficiency in English at the Upper Intermediate level or higher.
  • Strong analytical and creative skills demonstrated by past results.
  • Experience analyzing key metrics of marketing campaigns.
  • Research skills and ability to write technical tasks for designers.
  • Copywriting skills and experience working with international markets are advantageous.

Nice to have

  • Experience in video and UGC content creation.
  • Familiarity with web and app marketing.
  • B2C niche market experience.
  • Creative testing experience with Meta.
